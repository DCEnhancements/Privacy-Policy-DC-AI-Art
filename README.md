# Privacy Policy for DC AI Art

## 1. Introduction

DC Enhancements ("we", "our", "us") operates the **DC AI Art** application.  
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app and its paid or subscription features.

By using DC AI Art, you agree to the terms outlined here.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- Account details (email, username, password if applicable, or third-party login)  
- AI Art inputs, prompts, and preferences (styles, parameters, uploaded images)  
- Payment and subscription details (through external providers)  
- Communication with support (messages, inquiries, etc.)

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

- Device information (model, OS version, device ID)  
- Usage analytics (which features you use, session duration, etc.)  
- Crash reports and error logs  
- Approximate location (country/city-level)  
- Interaction data (ads or in-app promotions, if applicable)

---

## 3. Payments & Subscriptions

- Payments are handled securely via third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe, Google Play, Apple App Store, PayPal).  
- We **do not** store full credit card or bank information.  
- We only receive essential transaction info such as status, amount, and timestamp.  
- Subscription data (status, plan type, renewal date) is stored to manage your access to premium features.  
- Subscriptions may automatically renew unless canceled before the renewal date.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Provide and improve the DC AI Art app  
- Enable and manage paid/subscription features  
- Analyze app usage and performance  
- Communicate updates, offers, and service notices  
- Detect and fix bugs or issues  
- Ensure compliance with laws and payment regulations  

---

## 5. Data Processing & Third Parties

### 5.1 Local Processing
- AI prompts, images, and presets are stored **locally** whenever possible.  
- Sensitive data (like login tokens) are encrypted and not shared externally.

### 5.2 Third-Party Services

We use external services for:
- **Analytics & Performance Monitoring** (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics)  
- **Cloud Databases** for syncing or backup of user data  
- **Payment Processing** via trusted gateways  
- **Advertising & Promotions**, if included in the app  

Each third party only receives the minimum required data to perform their function.

---

## 6. Sharing Your Data

We only share your information in the following cases:
- When legally required (e.g., to comply with court orders or regulations)  
- To enforce our Terms of Service or prevent fraud  
- When you **explicitly choose to share**, such as publishing a public preset or artwork  

We never sell your data to advertisers or third parties.

---

## 7. Data Security

We take data protection seriously and use:
- End-to-end encryption (HTTPS, AES-256)  
- Secure data storage and authentication  
- Regular security audits and updates  
- Restricted employee access to user data  

However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

## 8. Data Retention & Deletion

- Cloud data (e.g., synced presets): remains until you delete it  
- Transaction records: stored only as long as legally required  
- You can request full account and data deletion anytime by contacting us
